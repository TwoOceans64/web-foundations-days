# Reflection

The most difficult concept in the course was **scaling systems under heavy load**. At first, I struggled to understand how caches, replicas, and queues interact without breaking consistency. I overcame this by carefully studying examples like QuickNotes and SnapShare, and practicing with diagrams that show request flows. Visualizing the arrows helped me see why separation of reads, writes, and background jobs is essential.

For my capstone, the feedback highlighted that my data model was solid but my architecture diagram lacked clarity on failure points. If I could improve one part, I would add redundancy and explain how components recover from crashes. This would make the design more realistic and resilient.

Next, I want to learn **API development and scalable automation**. Building real endpoints and handling concurrency will deepen my understanding of how theory translates into practice. I also plan to explore **cloud deployment** so I can test these designs under real traffic. This will prepare me to contribute meaningfully to Africa’s growing Fourth Industrial Revolution.
